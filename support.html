Velvet Rope Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report content? We're here to help.

Contact us

Email: cgcostas@gmail.com
We aim to reply within 2 business days. Reports of objectionable content are handled within 24 hours.

You can also send a message from the App: Profile → Report a problem.

Report a review or user

  • Tap ⚑ Report under any review and choose a reason.
  • To stop seeing a user's reviews, tap Report and then Block. You can manage blocked users under Profile → Blocked users.
  • For urgent safety concerns, email us with the venue name and the reviewer's username.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Velvet Rope and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, username, reviews, reports, blocks and saved venues right away, and it cannot be undone. If you can't sign in, email cgcostas@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

FAQ

Who can use Velvet Rope? Only adults aged 21 and over.

A venue's details are wrong or it has closed. Email us the venue name and the correct information.

I forgot my password. Email us from the address on your account, and we'll help you regain access.

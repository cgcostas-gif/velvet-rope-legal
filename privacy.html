This Privacy Policy explains how Chris Costas, an individual ("we", "us"), collects, uses and shares information when you use the Velvet Rope mobile app (the "App"). Velvet Rope is a review platform for adults aged 21 and over.

On your device only, the App stores your 21+ age confirmation and your sign-in session, and briefly uses your location (if you allow it) to show distances to clubs. If you use the App without an account, saved venues, blocks and any reviews you write are stored only on your device.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location. If you tap "Near me", the App asks for location access while you are using it (never in the background) and uses your position only on your device to sort clubs by distance. Your location is never sent to us or our providers, and it is not stored. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, health, financial or payment information. The App has no payments.

3. What is public

Your username, and the reviews you post together with their ratings and dates, are visible to everyone using the App. If you are a verified club owner, your responses (labeled "Response from the owner", with dates) and the venue's "Verified owner" badge are also public. Do not include personal information about yourself or others in reviews or responses. Your email address, club claim details (name, role, business phone and email, note), reports, blocks, saved venues and support messages are not public; claim details are seen only by our administrators.

4. How we share information

  • Service providers: we use Supabase (database, authentication and hosting) to run the App. Supabase processes data for us under its own terms and security practices.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers: if the App is sold or transferred, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, username, reviews, reports, blocks, saved venues, club claims, verified-owner status and owner responses from our database right away. Copies in server logs and backups kept by our provider may remain for a limited period until they expire. You can also ask for deletion by emailing cgcostas@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict some processing. Email cgcostas@gmail.com to make a request, and we will respond within the time the law requires. California residents, see section 7.

7. Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know and access: to ask what personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, why we collect it, and the categories of third parties we disclose it to, and to get a copy of it.
  • Right to delete: to ask us to delete the personal information we collected from you, with limited legal exceptions. You can also do this yourself at any time with Profile → Delete account.
  • Right to correct: to ask us to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: we use your account login (email and password) only to provide the App, so the right to limit doesn't apply.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or give you a different quality of service because you exercised any of these rights.

Categories collected in the past 12 months: identifiers (email address, username, account ID; plus IP address in server logs), your reviews, owner responses and support messages, professional or business contact information if you claim a venue (name, role, business phone and email), and your in-app activity (saved venues, blocks, reports), as described in section 1. We collect them from you and your device, and we use them for the purposes in section 1. We disclose them only to our service provider (Supabase) for business purposes. Usernames and reviews are public by design.

How to make a request: email cgcostas@gmail.com with the subject "California privacy request", say which right you want to use, and send it from the email address on your account. We may ask you for more information to verify your identity before we act. An authorized agent can make a request for you with your written permission. We will confirm we received your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this by up to another 45 days and will tell you if we do.

8. Age restriction

The App is only for people aged 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to our database is restricted per user by row-level security rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

10. International users

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our provider operates.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make a material change, we will let you know in the App or by email before it takes effect.
